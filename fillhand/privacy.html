Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Fillhand stores your macros, autofill profiles, spreadsheets and settings only in your browser (chrome.storage). If you turn on sync, macros and profiles (without values marked sensitive) are copied through your own Google/Chrome account by Chrome sync; Fillhand never sees them.

We have no servers and no accounts, and we do not collect analytics. When you activate a licence key, the key is sent to Polar (polar.sh), our payment provider, to verify it; Polar's privacy policy applies to purchases. Password fields are never recorded.

When you uninstall, Chrome deletes all of Fillhand's data and opens a one-question survey page; answering it is optional and opens a GitHub issue with only what you typed, which you review before posting (issues are public).
