Guide

Open each link in a spreadsheet, change something, save — and collect results

A column of record URLs (orders, tickets, listings) and the same edit to make on each. Or a value to read from each page into your sheet.

What you need: Chrome, the free Fillhand extension and your sheet with a URL column. No account, no code. Your data never leaves your computer.

Step by step

  1. Record the change on one record page.
  2. Edit the first step (Go to page) and set the URL to {{row.URL}}.
  3. Add Read value steps for anything you want back (status, total, new ID). They appear as columns in the results.
  4. Import the sheet on Data, attach it to the macro, and run.
  5. Export results as CSV — your original columns, plus the values read, plus ✓/✗ per row.

Tips that save a rerun

  • Log in to the site once before the run; Fillhand uses your existing session.
  • For thousands of links, run in batches with Start at row so you can check progress.
