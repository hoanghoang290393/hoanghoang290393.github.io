Submit a Google Form once for every row of a spreadsheet

Paper surveys typed into a sheet, registrations from an event, inspection checklists: when the answers already sit in a spreadsheet, you don't need to click through the form 200 times.

What you need: Chrome, the free Fillhand extension and your sheet exported as CSV or .xlsx (File → Download in Google Sheets). No account, no code. Your data never leaves your computer.

Step by step

  1. Install Fillhand and pin it. Click the toolbar icon to open the side panel.
  2. Open the Google Form in a tab. In the panel, choose Record new and fill the form once by hand, exactly as you normally would — type, pick from dropdowns, tick boxes, click Save.
  3. Click Stop. Each action is now a step you can read and edit.
  4. Go to Data and import your CSV or Excel (.xlsx) file. The first row should hold the column names.
  5. Open the macro, set Data source to your file, and replace each typed value with a placeholder: click the value, then {{row.Email}}, {{row.Company}} … (the panel lists your columns).
  6. Add a final Go to page step with the form's link, or record the “Submit another response” click, so every row starts on an empty form.
  7. Press Run. Fillhand repeats the macro once per row and shows ✓ or ✗ for each one.
  8. If a row fails, the panel shows which step and why. Fix it and press Run failed and remaining rows — rows already done are not entered twice.
  9. Export the results as CSV (with a status column) if you need a record of what was entered.

Tips that save a rerun

  • Multiple-choice and checkbox questions: put the exact option text in the cell (Strongly agree; several as A; B).
  • Required questions that are empty in a row make that row fail with the form's own message — fix the cell and re-run the failed rows.
  • Only submit responses you are entitled to submit; Fillhand types what is in your sheet, nothing more.
