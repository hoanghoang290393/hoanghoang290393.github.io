How autofill profiles work

  1. Start from a template (my details, shipping address, company details) or open the form and press Capture this form.
  2. Optionally limit the profile to a site, e.g. https://portal.example.com/*.
  3. Turn on Fill automatically when the page opens, or press Alt+Shift+F / right-click → Fill.

Moving your old rules

Most autofill extensions can export their profiles. Open the exported file, copy the field/value pairs into a Fillhand profile (field names are matched by label, name, placeholder and autocomplete, so you rarely need selectors). Need help moving a big set? Open a support request with the export (remove anything private) and we will convert it.
