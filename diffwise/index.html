Common questions

Which files can I compare? PDF (text or scanned), Word (.docx, .doc), OpenDocument (.odt), RTF, HTML, plain text, photos and camera scans.

A document is split into several files. Add them all to the same side — Diffwise orders them by article number and tells you if a part is missing.

Some words on a scan are marked as uncertain. Tap the banner to confirm or correct them; Diffwise compares again once.

Restore a purchase. Open Settings (gear on the home screen) › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancel a subscription. iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Diffwise.

Is my document uploaded? No. Everything runs on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
