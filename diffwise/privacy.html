Diffwise Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026 · Diffwise: Compare Documents for iPhone and iPad

Short version: Diffwise does not collect any data. Your documents are read, compared and stored only on your device. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no analytics.

1. Your documents

Files you open, text you paste, pages you scan with the camera and photos you pick are processed entirely on your iPhone or iPad. Text recognition for scanned pages runs on the device. Nothing is uploaded to the developer or to any cloud service, and no AI service outside your device sees your text.

Saved comparisons are kept in the app's local storage. Files you put in the Diffwise folder (Files › On My iPhone › Diffwise) stay there until you delete them. Exported reports (PDF, Word, Excel, text) go only where you choose to share or save them.

2. Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a paper document. Photos are read only when you pick them. Images are not sent anywhere.

3. Purchases

Diffwise Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Payment is handled by Apple; the developer does not receive your name, email or payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple's StoreKit on your device. A small counter of free comparisons used is stored on your device.

4. What is not collected

  • No personal information, contacts, location or identifiers.
  • No advertising, tracking or third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No account or sign-in.

If you choose to share crash data with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports under Apple's own privacy policy.

5. Children

Diffwise is a general-audience productivity app and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

6. Your choices

Delete a comparison by swiping it in Recent, or delete everything by removing the app. Manage purchases and subscriptions in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated before the change takes effect in the app.
